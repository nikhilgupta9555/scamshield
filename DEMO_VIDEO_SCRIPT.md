## 🎙️ Recording Tips for the Team
1. **Resolution:** Record screen at 1080p (1920x1080) at 60fps using OBS Studio or Chrome Screen Recorder.
2. **Audio:** Use a clean microphone with noise cancellation.
3. **Pacing:** Keep interactions snappy—use the 1-Click Presets for instantaneous results.
